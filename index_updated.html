About

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the New Jersey Institute of Technology. My research focuses on computer architecture, DRAM-based computing, processing-in-memory, hardware security, RowHammer and RowPress attacks, and secure AI systems.

My work spans architecture design, simulation, FPGA-based DRAM characterization, and the development of defense mechanisms for protecting deep neural network models and memory systems.

Selected Research Projects

FlexiDRAM — ISLPED 2022

A flexible processing-in-DRAM framework for parallel general-purpose computation, achieving substantial energy savings and performance improvements over GPU execution.

ReD-LUT — ICCAD 2022 and 2023

A reconfigurable in-DRAM lookup-table architecture for massively parallel logic, arithmetic, deep-learning, and data-encryption workloads.

LT-PIM — IEEE Computer Architecture Letters

A lookup-table-based processing-in-DRAM architecture with integrated RowHammer self-tracking.

P-PIM — DATE 2023

A parallel processing-in-DRAM framework that combines efficient computation with low-overhead RowHammer protection.

DRAM-Locker — DATE 2024

A general-purpose DRAM protection mechanism using in-DRAM swapping and lock-table management to defend DNN weights against adversarial attacks.

DNN-Defender — DAC 2024

A victim-focused in-DRAM defense framework that protects DNN models against consecutive targeted RowHammer bit-flip attacks.

Threshold Break — IEEE TCAD

An experimental study of escalating RowHammer attack distance to evaluate the limitations of counter-based defense mechanisms.

Targeted RowPress — DATE 2025

An evaluation of targeted RowPress attacks and their impact on deep neural network accuracy and model reliability.
